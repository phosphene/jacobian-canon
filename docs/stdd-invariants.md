# Scientific Test-Driven Development (STDD) & Invariant Contracts

**Scientific Test-Driven Development (STDD)** establishes verification over continuous mathematical properties rather than point-wise oracle snapshots. Under this discipline, test suites verify continuous invariant contracts across parameter spaces:
1. Validating global structural behavior across continuous function domains.
2. Testing mathematical symmetries, dualities, and conservation laws directly.
3. Distinguishing algorithmic convergence from coincidental error cancellation.

`jaccanon` implements STDD by pairing deterministic boundary tests with property-based invariant verification (`Hypothesis`).

---

## 1. The 4 Invariant Contracts

### Contract 1: Taylor Remainder Convergence
For any valid Jacobian candidate $J$ and perturbation $v$ with $\|v\| < \delta$:
$$R(v) = \|f(x + v) - f(x) - J \cdot v\|$$
Must satisfy:
$$\lim_{\|v\| \to 0} \frac{R(v)}{\|v\|} = 0, \quad \text{and} \quad R(v) \le C \cdot \|v\|^2$$

**Verification Harness:** Geometric step sweeps verifying that the empirical slope of $\log R(v)$ versus $\log \|v\|$ approaches exactly $2.0$.

---

### Contract 2: Adjoint Duality (Pushforward vs. Pullback)
For all directional test vectors $v \in \mathbb{R}^n$ and covectors $u \in \mathbb{R}^m$:
$$\langle u, \text{JVP}(f, x, v) \rangle \equiv \langle \text{VJP}(f, x, u), v \rangle$$

**Verification Harness:** Evaluates forward pushforward and reverse pullback across random $(u, v)$ pairs generated by Hypothesis; verifies inner product equality to within floating-point tolerance:
$$|\langle u, J v \rangle - \langle J^T u, v \rangle| \le 10 \cdot \epsilon_{\text{mach}} \cdot \|u\|_2 \|v\|_2 \|J\|_F$$

---

### Contract 3: Helmholtz Orthogonality & Decomposition Uniqueness
For any square Jacobian $M \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$:
$$S = \frac{1}{2}(M + M^T), \quad A = \frac{1}{2}(M - M^T)$$
Must satisfy:
1. **Exact Reconstruction:** $S + A \equiv M$
2. **Frobenius Orthogonality:** $\langle S, A \rangle_F = \text{tr}(S^T A) = \sum_{i,j} S_{ij} A_{ij} \equiv 0$

**Verification Harness:** Property tests generating arbitrary random matrices across dynamic ranges $[-10^4, 10^4]$ and verifying orthogonality to floating-point $\epsilon$.

---

### Contract 4: Holomorphic Consistency (Lyness-Moler vs. Dual Numbers)
For real-analytic functions within their holomorphic domain:
$$\|J_{\text{dual}} - J_{\text{complex-step}}\|_\infty \le 5 \cdot \epsilon_{\text{mach}} \cdot \|J_{\text{dual}}\|_\infty$$

**Verification Harness:** Confirms that the complex-step coordinate stencil evaluates identically to forward dual-number arithmetic within machine precision, certifying zero subtractive cancellation.
